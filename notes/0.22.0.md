- **A question reaches you as a question.** When a session stops to ask you something, the Inbox shows the question, with its choices, instead of "turn ended", and you can answer it right there.
- **Stalls are no longer "finished".** A session that stops without saying it finished or asking you anything shows as "Stopped without finishing", with its own sound. If you watched it stop, or stopped it yourself with Esc, it stays a plain finished turn.
