Kind: feature

- **Adopted work brings its Codex conversation along.** When you adopt a merge or pull request in Kehikot, Codex says which of this project's conversations worked on its branch, mentioned it, or ran in this repository, and one that worked on its branch can be filed under the place you adopt it into, as typing $kehikot-here would. A conversation already kept for another place is never moved, and the undo takes it back.
- **Kehikot can start a Codex conversation for a task.** A task Kehikot runs for you starts a new Codex conversation in the project with its brief as the first prompt, filed under the task's place, and Kehikot can show that one conversation on its own while it works.
