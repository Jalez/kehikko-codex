Kind: feature

- **Adopting an item with Codex no longer fails with “did not answer within 8 s”.** Codex now takes the task at once and starts afterwards; the dialog closes straight away and the conversation appears in the Codex container as it starts. Before, Codex had to boot and name its conversation first, which could take longer than Kehikot waits — and the conversation started anyway, unseen.
- **If Codex cannot start the task, the Inbox says why.**
- **A conversation placed with $kehikot-here can now say how its turn ends.** `tell_finished` takes the conversation's key, so its Inbox line shows done, a decision for you, or research complete.
