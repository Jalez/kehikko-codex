Kind: feature

- **After a restart, a conversation that was busy carries on by itself.** One that was working, or whose turn ended while its own background work was still running, is woken in the background with one sentence saying why Kehikot restarted (an update says which version) and to carry on. Before, it was only told when you next opened its place, so you had to visit each one and say "continue". Idle and finished conversations, and ones waiting for you, are left alone.
