- **Work in folders beside the project.** A new setting, "Other folders sessions may work in", lists folders outside the project that this project's sessions may also work in: Codex can edit there as it does in the project. Commands that leave the sandbox still ask as your approvals setting says.
- **Only sensible folders.** A folder must exist; the whole disk, your home folder, system folders and places that hold your keys or accounts are refused with a sentence saying why.
- **Restart when you are ready.** A change applies when a session starts or restarts, and a running session offers the restart.
